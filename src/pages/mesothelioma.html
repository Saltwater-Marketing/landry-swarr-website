Legal Options for Financial Help

And while a lawsuit does not help the health of asbestos workers or family members, there is financial recourse for the victims of Mesothelioma. The Malignant Mesothelioma law firm of Landry & Swarr has fought for the rights and families of those afflicted by this devastating disease.

In fact, for the past century, these types of lawsuits have sprung up all around the country as negligent managers and business owners have paid for the ‘cost cuts that ended up costing lives.’

The first Mesothelioma litigator took action in the year 1929. It was then that businesses were first held accountable for exposing employees to abnormally high levels of asbestos.

In the years following this first lawsuit, lawyers all over the country have been fighting on behalf of these poor men and women who are in desperate need of proper defense. The main points of these lawsuits simply pertain to the fact that these employees were working for businesses that operated in an asbestos infested work environment. The lack of awareness, and an inability to implement safety measures once Mesothelioma and other asbestos related problems were brought to the attention of the public, has led to many victories for the afflicted, totaling billions of damages in awards to families.

Unfortunately, in this day and age, many people who are now filing lawsuits do not actually suffer from Mesothelioma, but rather are suing as a ‘precaution.’ As a result, many of the courts are clogged up with thousands of meaningless lawsuits. This isn’t to say that Mesothelioma is no longer a problem. Quite the contrary, in fact, as more than 10,000 people a year die from asbestos related diseases. Luckily, many Mesothelioma litigators look out for the afflicted and their families, allowing them to deal with the financial burdens of medical expenses and salaries lost.
