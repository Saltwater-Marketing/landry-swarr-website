Nearly all cases of mesothelioma are malignant, or cancer causing. In fact, those that were previously defined as benign have been reclassified by most medical professionals under a different and distinct name. Each year, between two and three thousand cases of mesothelioma are diagnosed annually.

Types of malignant mesothelioma

In general, there are three types of malignant mesothelioma: pleural, peritoneal, and pericardial. Some people consider a fourth type, testicular mesothelioma, but the occurrence of this form is so rare that it is frequently excluded from discussion of mesothelioma in general.

  • Pleural mesothelioma affects the lungs, the lining surrounding the lungs, and the cavity surrounding the lungs; this is the most common form accounting for approximately 75% of total mesothelioma cases
  • Peritoneal mesothelioma affects the abdomen, the lining of the abdomen and cavity surrounding the abdomen; the second most common type of mesothelioma, this type accounts for ten to twenty percent of total cases
  • Pericardial mesothelioma affects the heart, tissue surrounding the heart, and the heart cavity

Malignant mesothelioma treatment

Each type of malignant mesothelioma is deadly. Three common treatments include surgery, chemotherapy, and radiation. However, these treatments are more successful on patients who are diagnosed earlier, and that is rarely the case for mesothelioma patients.

Causes of mesothelioma

There is only one prove cause of malignant (cancer causing) melanoma: ongoing exposure to asbestos. However, most patients do not show symptoms of the diseases until twenty to even fifty years after their initial exposure. For this reason, the disease is often untreated for a significant amount of time, resulting in lower survival rates.
