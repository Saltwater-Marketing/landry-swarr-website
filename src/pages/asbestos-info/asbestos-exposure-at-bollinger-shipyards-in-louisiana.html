Bollinger Shipyards specializes in the construction, repair, and conversion of an array of commercial and military vessels. The 13 facilities operated by Bollinger Shipyards are located along the Louisiana Gulf Cost, Texas Gulf Coast, and the Mississippi River. And unfortunately, some of the workers at the Bollinger Shipyard in Louisiana may have been exposed to asbestos during their time of employment, especially if it was before 1980.

Founded in 1946, Bollinger Shipyards continues to be owned and operated by the Bollinger family. Since 1984, the company has supplied the United States Coast Guard and Navy with all of their patrol boats. Bollinger currently services deepwater vessels and shallow-draft vessels, in addition to operating 40 dry docks.

Where Exposure May Have Occurred

Bollinger Shipyards makes a concerted effort at ensuring the safety of the environment and its workers. However, it was once very common for companies, including Bollinger Shipyards, to use asbestos, which caused a great number of workers to contract asbestos-related diseases such as mesothelioma or asbestosis.
